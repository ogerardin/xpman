# Aircraft

![Aircraft panel showing aircraft cards and available actions](assets/screenshots/aircraft.png)
*The Aircraft panel.*

## Find and install aircraft

Choose **Reload** to rescan aircraft. Use **Search** to filter by name, author, or studio, and **Filter** to show a category or studio. Choose **Install** to install an aircraft from an archive; see [Installing add-ons](09-installing-addons.md).

Each card shows the aircraft name, author or studio, version, and available update action. Expand the livery section to browse liveries. Double-click a card to inspect the aircraft's ACF properties.

## Inspect and edit an aircraft

Hover over a card to reveal its actions. Choose **Inspect** to review findings, **Reveal** to open the aircraft folder, or **Properties** from the context menu to edit details such as the callsign, tail number, ICAO code, and notes.

## Uninstall an aircraft

Choose **Uninstall** on the card and review the confirmation before proceeding. If multiple aircraft variants share a folder, use **Uninstall this variant** to remove only the selected variant. Shared files and liveries remain in place. Use **Uninstall aircraft** to remove the aircraft and review the items listed in the confirmation.

## Other actions

Right-click a card or livery to reveal its folder, inspect it, open available links or manuals, or edit properties. On macOS, **Remove macOS quarantine** is available for quarantined aircraft and can allow bundled plugins to load.
